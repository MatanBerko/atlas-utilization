Note: an earlier run of this same script also compared matched-EXCLUSIVE
directly against generic-EXCLUSIVE and reported FAIL (373 and 72
signatures existing only in matched-exclusive for the two SingleMuon
files). This is NOT a bug: it is the expected, by-design consequence of
Step 2's own exclusive-shard rule for SingleMuon in matched mode (veto
ONLY against DoubleMuon's ACCEPTANCE) being a genuinely different,
narrower veto than generic mode's (veto against DoubleMuon/DoubleEG/
MuonEG's trigger BITS) -- an event vetoed in generic mode by DoubleEG or
MuonEG firing (not DoubleMuon) is correctly NOT vetoed in matched mode,
since electron datasets are explicitly out of scope for the matched-mode
combination. The corrected, meaningful checks (matched-inclusive subset
of generic-inclusive; matched-exclusive subset of matched-inclusive) both
PASS for all 4 pilot files, and together prove by transitivity that
matched-exclusive is also a subset of generic-inclusive.
