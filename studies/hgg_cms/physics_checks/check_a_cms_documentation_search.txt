Check A, step 4: search of CMS NanoAOD / EGM documentation for an
explicit statement on whether MC photon (and electron) energies already
have smearing corrections applied in the NanoAOD versions used here
(UL2016 NanoAODv9, i.e. "up to NanoAODv10" in the terminology below).

Source: CMS WorkBookNanoAOD, CMS Public TWiki,
https://twiki.cern.ch/twiki/bin/view/CMSPublic/WorkBookNanoAOD
(fetched 2026-09-15; see also the linked EG POG NanoAOD TWiki,
https://twiki.cern.ch/twiki/bin/view/CMS/EgammaNanoAOD, not separately
fetched in this task).

Quoted directly (Photons section):

  "Up to NanoAOD10, residual energy scale and resolution corrections are
  applied to the stored electrons to match the data."
  [the Photons section of the same page states the equivalent for
  photons: "... applied to the stored photons to match the data."]

  "The original four-momentum (as stored in MiniAOD) can be obtained by
  rescaling by the reciprocal of Photon_eCorr."

  "Starting from NanoAODv11, the corrections need to be applied on top of
  NanoAOD using correctionlib."

INTERPRETATION: this is an explicit, primary CMS statement that BOTH an
energy SCALE correction (data) and a RESOLUTION correction (i.e.
smearing, for MC) are already applied to the stored Photon_pt in the
NanoAOD version family used here (v9, "up to NanoAOD10"). This directly
supports (and was found independently of) this task's own empirical
measurement in check_a_smearing.py: MC Photon_eCorr has ~2x data's RMS
spread with a mean consistent with 1 (a smearing signature), and the
truth-matched response distribution is measurably WIDER after Photon_eCorr
is applied than before, in every photon category tested.
